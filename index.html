01 — About

A small team, real products

Godopetza Labs is a software studio based in Dar es Salaam. We design and build innovative, full-stack solutions for businesses across East Africa, and ship our own products live on Google Play and the App Store.

We work in modern stacks with an agile, ship-often approach — the kind of software that handles payments, stories, weddings, and everyday money, and holds up in production.
